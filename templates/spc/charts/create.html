Chart Type Guide
X-bar and R Chart

Use when measuring subgroups of 2-10 items. Tracks both the average and range of subgroups over time.

X-bar and S Chart

Use when measuring subgroups of more than 10 items. Uses standard deviation instead of range.

I-MR Chart

Use when measuring individual items. Moving range tracks variation between consecutive items.

C Chart

Use when counting the number of defects in a constant sample size.

P Chart

Use when tracking the proportion (percentage) of defective items.

U Chart

Use when tracking defects per unit when sample sizes vary.
