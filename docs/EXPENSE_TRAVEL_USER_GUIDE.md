# Expense & Travel Management - User Guide

## Overview

The Expense & Travel Management module provides a comprehensive, self-service solution for employees to submit expense claims, request travel authorizations, manage cash advances, and track reimbursements.

---

## For Employees

### Submitting an Expense Claim

1. **Navigate** to Expense & Travel → Claims → New Claim
2. **Enter** basic information:
   - Claim date
   - Business purpose
   - Department (auto-filled from profile)
3. **Add** line items:
   - Category (select from dropdown)
   - Description
   - Amount
   - Currency
   - Receipt (optional but recommended)
   - Cost allocation (optional)
4. **Submit** the claim for approval

**Tips**:
- Attach receipts for all items over $25
- Use descriptive business purpose
- Select correct expense category

---

### Travel Request Process

1. **Navigate** to Expense & Travel → Travel → New Travel Request
2. **Enter** trip details:
   - Destination (city, country)
   - Start date and end date
   - Purpose of trip
   - Estimated costs by category
3. **Add** itinerary segments:
   - Flight/train/bus details
   - Accommodation details
   - Ground transportation
4. **Attach** supporting documents (invitation, meeting agenda)
5. **Submit** for approval

---

### Cash Advance Request

1. **Navigate** to Expense & Travel → Cash Advances → New Advance
2. **Enter** advance details:
   - Amount needed
   - Currency
   - Purpose
   - Expected settlement date
3. **Submit** for approval
4. **Track** approval status in My Advances

**Note**: Advances must be settled within 30 days of trip completion.

---

### Checking Claim Status

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Claims
2. View status in the list:
   - 🟡 Draft - Not yet submitted
   - 🔵 Submitted - Awaiting approval
   - 🟢 Approved - Ready for payment
   - 🟣 Paid - Reimbursement processed
   - 🔴 Rejected - Not approved
   - 🟠 Returned - Need corrections

3. Click on claim number to see full details and history

---

### Receipt Management

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Receipts
2. Click "Upload Receipt"
3. Drag and drop or browse for file
4. Link receipt to expense claim

**Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, PDF (max 10MB)

---

## For Approvers

### Reviewing Pending Claims

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Approvals
2. View list of items awaiting your approval
3. Click on item to see full details:
   - Employee information
   - Expense breakdown
   - Receipts attached
   - Policy violations highlighted
   - Approval history

4. **Actions**:
   - ✅ Approve - Move to next step
   - ❌ Reject - End process with reason
   - ↩️ Return - Send back for corrections
   - ⏭️ Delegate - Assign to another approver

---

### Bulk Approvals

For efficiency, use bulk approval:
1. Select multiple items using checkboxes
2. Click "Approve Selected" or "Reject Selected"
3. Confirm action

---

### Setting Up Delegations

If you will be unavailable:
1. Navigate to Settings → Expense & Travel Settings → My Delegations
2. Click "Add Delegation"
3. Select delegate (another approver)
4. Set start and end dates
5. Save

**Note**: You can still approve items while delegation is active; delegate sees same items.

---

## For Finance Team

### Processing Reimbursements

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Reimbursements
2. Filter for "Approved - Ready for Payment"
3. Select items for payment
4. Click "Create Reimbursement"
5. Generate payment file or post to accounting

---

### Journal Preview

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Finance → Journal Preview
2. Review accounting entries before posting
3. Adjust entries if needed
4. Post to general ledger

---

### Per Diem Configuration

1. Navigate to Expense & Travel → Settings → Per Diem Rules
2. Set daily rates by destination
3. Define meal and incidental allowances
4. Update annually for inflation

---

## Common Tasks

### How to Edit a Draft Claim
1. Go to Claims list
2. Find draft (🟡 status)
3. Click Edit icon
4. Make changes
5. Save

### How to Cancel a Submitted Claim
1. Go to Claims list
2. Click on submitted claim
3. Click "Withdraw" button
4. Confirm cancellation

**Note**: Cannot withdraw if already approved.

### How to Link a Receipt to Existing Claim
1. Go to Receipts
2. Find unreferenced receipt
3. Click "Link to Claim"
4. Select claim number
5. Confirm

### How to Check Policy Limits
1. Go to Policies
2. Find relevant policy
3. View category limits
4. Check if expense exceeds threshold

## Notifications

You will receive Flow notifications for:
- Claim submitted (to approvers)
- Claim approved/rejected (to employee)
- Approval reminder (if pending > 24h)
- Cash advance issued
- Reimbursement processed
- Delegation started/ended
