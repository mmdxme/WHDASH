MMDx چیست؟

MMDx یک سیستم جامع مدیریت انبار و کنترل موجودی است که برای سازمان‌هایی طراحی شده که چندین شرکت فرعی دارند. این سیستم دید کامل و کنترل بر موجودی، تحویل‌ها، حسابداری و عملیات در تمام مکان‌های شرکت فراهم می‌کند.

مزایای کلیدی

  • پیگیری موجودی لحظه‌ای در تمام انبارها
  • سابقه کامل از تمام جابجایی‌های انبار
  • پشتیبانی از چندین شرکت با گزارش‌دهی یکپارچه
  • مدیریت ساده تحویل و سفرها
  • یکپارچه‌سازی حسابداری و مدیریت مشتریان

شروع به کار

نحوه دسترسی به سیستم

  1. مرورگر وب خود را باز کنید (Chrome, Firefox, Edge یا Safari)
  2. به آدرس بروید http://localhost:5000 (محلی) یا آدرس سرور شما
  3. وارد شوید با نام کاربری و رمز عبور ارائه شده توسط مدیر
  4. ترجیحات خود را تنظیم کنید شامل تم (روشن/تاریک)، منطقه زمانی و گزینه‌های نمایش
  5. تنظیمات تحویل را پیکربندی کنید اگر مدیریت تحویل‌ها را انجام می‌دهید

راه‌اندازی اولیه

مرحله توضیحات موقعیت
۱. پیکربندی شرکت تنظیم اطلاعات شرکت و مکان‌های انبار منوی تنظیمات
۲. افزودن کاربران ایجاد حساب‌های کاربری و تخصیص نقش‌ها مدیریت کاربران
۳. تنظیمات قطعات تعریف دسته‌بندی‌ها، برندها و تنظیمات قطعات تنظیمات قطعات
۴. ورود قطعات آپلود داده‌های موجودی قطعات از طریق قالب اکسل داشبورد > ورود
۵. تنظیمات تحویل تنظیم رانندگان، خودروها و انواع فعالیت‌ها تنظیمات تحویل

Project Overview

What is MMDx?

MMDx is a comprehensive Warehouse Management and Inventory Control System designed for organizations managing multiple subsidiaries. It provides complete visibility and control over inventory, deliveries, accounting, and operations across all company locations.

Key Benefits

  • Real-time inventory tracking across all warehouses
  • Complete audit trail for all stock movements
  • Multi-company support with consolidated reporting
  • Streamlined delivery and trip management
  • Integrated accounting and customer management

Companies Using the System

The system supports multiple subsidiary companies:

Holding Company SDAD AFRA Carmania
Core Business Modules

Inventory Management

Parts Catalog

Manage your complete parts inventory with detailed information including part numbers, descriptions, weights, dimensions, categories, brands, and costs.

Stock Operations

Perform stock entries (receiving), stock exits (dispatching), and stock transfers between warehouses with full documentation.

Movement History

Complete audit trail tracking all inventory movements including entries, exits, adjustments, and transfers with timestamps and user records.

Warehouse Zones

Organize inventory across multiple warehouse locations and zones for efficient storage and retrieval.

Delivery Management

Trip Planning

Plan and manage delivery trips with multiple customer stops, route optimization, and driver assignments.

Fleet Management

Manage your vehicle fleet with maintenance tracking, capacity information, and availability status.

GPS Tracking

Real-time tracking of delivery vehicles with arrival and departure timestamps at each stop.

Activity Logging

Comprehensive activity logging for all delivery operations including check-ins, departures, and trip completions.

Customer & Accounting

Customer Database

Maintain detailed customer records with contact information, locations, working hours, and service preferences.

Account Management

Track customer balances, credit limits, and payment histories with real-time account status.

Invoices & Payments

Manage customer invoices, record payments, and maintain complete transaction history.

Transaction Records

Comprehensive transaction logging for all financial activities with detailed categorization.

Task & Issue Management

Task Tracking

Create and manage tasks organized by departments with priorities, assignments, and progress tracking.

Issue Tracking

Log and track issues with Pareto analysis capabilities to identify and prioritize recurring problems.

Follow-up System

Complete follow-up tracking with audit trails for both tasks and issues.

Department Organization

Organize issues and tasks by department for targeted resolution and accountability.

Getting Started

How to Access the System

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  2. Navigate to http://localhost:5000 (local) or your deployed URL
  3. Login with your username and password provided by your administrator
  4. Set your preferences including theme (dark/light mode), timezone, and display options
  5. Configure delivery settings if you will be managing deliveries

First Time Setup

Step Description Location
1. Configure Company Set up your company information and warehouse locations Settings Menu
2. Add Users Create user accounts and assign roles/permissions User Management
3. Set Parts Configuration Define categories, brands, and part settings Parts Settings
4. Import Parts Upload existing parts data via Excel template Dashboard > Import
5. Configure Delivery Set up drivers, vehicles, and activity types Delivery Settings
User Roles & Permissions

Role-Based Access Control

The system supports configurable roles with specific permissions for:

  • Administrators - Full system access including user management and settings
  • Managers - Access to all modules with limited user management
  • Warehouse Staff - Inventory and stock operations access
  • Delivery Staff - Delivery management and tracking access
  • Accountants - Accounting and customer account access
  • Viewers - Read-only access to reports and dashboards
